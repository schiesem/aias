AIAS Alignment Ontology: Description back to ToC

Nothing here is normative. The patterns rest on standards, this ontology rests on design decisions of this work, and every class and relation says which of the two it is. Two classes are related with owl:equivalentClass, and only where the definitions are word for word the same. Control is one of them: ISO/IEC 22989, 3.5.5 and IEC 60050-351, 351-42-19 carry the identical wording and trace back to the same source, which makes the two the same concept rather than similar ones. Everything else is a subclass relation or a relation of its own, because an equivalence claims more than an overlap supports. Two traps are recorded rather than resolved. Controllability appears in two patterns with the same spelling and different meanings, one a property of a dynamic system and one a property of an AI system, and they are deliberately not related. The actuator of IEC 60050-351 is the signal side driving a valve, not the valve, so the device class of this ontology matches the final controlling element instead. ALIGNMENT.md gives both in full.
