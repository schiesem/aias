VDI 3682 Ontology Design Pattern: Description back to ToC

Normative basis: VDI/VDE 3682 Part 1 and Part 2, issue May 2015. Relation to prior work: this pattern is built independently but adopts several design decisions from the VDI 3682 ODP of the Institute of Automation Technology at Helmut Schmidt University (https://github.com/hsu-aut/IndustrialStandard-ODP-VDI3682, MIT licence). Adopted are the property chains for predecessor, successor, sub process and upper process, the defined class axiom for ProcessOperator, the dependency property expressing conjunction between states under the open world assumption, and the attribute structure of Identification and Characteristic. Structural deviations from that pattern are deliberate and documented at the affected elements: flows are reified as classes rather than expressed as object properties, assignments are reified as classes, and the system limit carries an explicit containment relation. See the notes on Flow, Assignment and SystemLimit.
