ISO/IEC 7498-1 Pattern: Named Individuals: Description back to ToC

Three groups of individual, with different standing. The seven layers are normative: ISO/IEC 7498-1, 6.1.2 enumerates them and gives each a number. The protocol data units and the technologies are conventional. Frame, packet, and segment do not occur in the standard, and 1.3 states that it implies no particular technology. They are supplied because an engineering model needs them and because naming them once keeps models comparable. The list of technologies is open by design. The quality of service parameters follow 7.4.4 for the four the standard names, and extend beyond it where practice requires, which is marked at the affected individuals.
